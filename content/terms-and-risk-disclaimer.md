## 2. Risk Disclaimer

**This is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.** Nothing on the Site(s), in the Discord server, on X, or anywhere else the project publishes content constitutes a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold $ASHEM or any other asset.

**Cryptocurrencies and memecoins are extremely high-risk instruments.** Specific to $ASHEM and tokens like it:

- **You can lose all of the money you put in.** Token value can go to zero. There is no guarantee of liquidity — you may not be able to sell your tokens at any price, or at all.
- **Supply is deflationary by design**, which is a mechanic, not a promise of price appreciation. A shrinking supply does not guarantee a rising price; demand can fall faster than supply.
- **On-chain mechanics reduce specific risks (like mint-authority abuse or freeze-authority abuse) — they do not eliminate all risk.** Market risk, liquidity risk, smart-contract-ecosystem risk (Token-2022 itself, Solana network risk), and general crypto-market risk all still apply in full.
- **Regulatory status is uncertain and varies by jurisdiction.** Laws around tokens like $ASHEM are evolving and differ by country/state. It is your responsibility to determine whether interacting with $ASHEM is legal where you live. $ASHEM does not restrict access by jurisdiction and does not represent that it is compliant with the laws of any specific country or region.
- **No one associated with $ASHEM is registered as a financial advisor, broker-dealer, or investment adviser** in any jurisdiction. Nothing here should be treated as advice from a licensed professional.

### Recommendations to reduce risk (not a guarantee against loss)
- Never commit more than you are fully prepared to lose.
- Verify every on-chain claim yourself before acting — mint authority, freeze authority, fee authority, and supply are all independently checkable on Solscan; links are provided in `/verificar-mint`, `/contrato`, and `#onchain-verification`.
- Confirm you are using the official mint address and official links (`/links` in Discord, or the pinned links in `#announcements`) before connecting a wallet or interacting with any contract.
- Never approve a token/wallet permission you do not understand. When in doubt, don't sign.
- Assume unsolicited DMs offering "support," "verification," or "giveaways" are scams (see Section 3).

## 3. Safety Guide — how we will (and will never) contact you

- **The $ASHEM team will never DM you first.** If you receive an unsolicited direct message from someone claiming to be $ASHEM staff, support, or a moderator, it is not us.
- **We will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet password — for any reason, including "verification," "support," or "unlocking a reward."** No legitimate request ever requires this. Anyone who asks is a scammer.
- **There is no staking, no whitelist, and no "connect your wallet here" flow outside the official dApp** at `ashem-demo.vercel.app` (mirrored via `ashem.xyz`).
- If you believe someone is impersonating the project or attempting to phish you, report it immediately: use `/reportar-scam` in Discord (opens a private thread visible only to you and staff) or flag it publicly — do not engage with the scammer, do not click links they send.
- Before trusting any claim about $ASHEM's mechanics (mint authority, freeze authority, supply, treasury), verify it yourself. Every number we publish is checkable on-chain — that is the entire point of the project ("we don't ask for trust — we publish proof").

## 5. Why this document exists

This document serves two purposes at once, and both are intentional:

1. **It protects you** — by giving you real, specific information about the risks involved and how to avoid the most common ways people lose money to scams in crypto communities.
2. **It protects the project** — by making clear, in writing, that $ASHEM does not offer financial advice, does not promise returns, and has communicated the risks involved.
